## Protected Semantics

- One CEO instruction creates at most one `ExecutionBatch`.
- Perpetual and spot cumulative external fills never exceed their respective CEO inputs.
- Each newly proven perpetual cumulative-fill increment releases at most its proportional, quantized spot increment; replayed `execId` values release nothing.
- Spot release remains in the same business batch and uses deterministic child identity.
- A cancel acknowledgement is not terminal evidence. Repost is forbidden until terminal cancellation is proven.
- Disconnect, sequence/identity mismatch, unknown order state or `result_unknown` stops new side effects and retains unknown state for reconciliation.
- Global strategy open/close execution batches are serialized. Multiple already-held funding pairs do not relax this rule.
- Platform and Runtime Live Write remain independently disabled by default; every future window requires separate owner authorization and forced read-only reset.
- Owner-approved initial repository parameters are 15-second TTL, one-second evaluation cadence, at most five mutations, and at least one tick price movement. They are configuration, not exchange facts or permanent production defaults.
