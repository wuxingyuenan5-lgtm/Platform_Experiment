## Version objective

Complete two local CEO-operated strategy closures with verified real data, controlled execution and auditable records:

- Cross-venue spread: existing Bybit `XAUTUSDT` perpetual leg and Bybit-provided MT5 `XAUUSD+` gold leg.
- Funding carry: one Bybit account, CEO-specified perpetual-short plus spot-long pairs, supporting multiple concurrently held symbols.

Both strategies reuse one bounded two-leg command, execution-batch, idempotency, ledger, reconciliation and recovery foundation. They must record instructions, external orders, fills or deals, positions, fees, funding, PnL/NAV, operating state and reconciliation evidence.

## Approved product decisions

- Cross-spread remains limited to the existing gold pair for 0.11.1.
- Funding carry uses perpetual short plus spot long only; reverse carry and cross-venue funding carry are excluded.
- The CEO manually enters both symbols and both quantities and owns economic sizing. The platform does not select symbols, size, rebalance or silently alter quantities.
- Funding open and close execute the perpetual leg first with bounded PostOnly chase, then the spot leg only after authoritative first-leg disposition. It never silently falls back to market.
- Funding acceptance crosses at least one real funding settlement and reconciles the resulting external ledger fact.
- Multiple funding pairs may be held concurrently, while new open or close execution batches remain serialized.
- One CEO instruction creates at most one execution batch. Child amend or cancel-repost activity cannot increase cumulative fill beyond the instruction quantity. Duplicate submission, restart, timeout, disconnect or `result_unknown` cannot create a second intent.
- Funding partial fills use incremental hedging: each newly proven perpetual cumulative-fill increment releases only the corresponding proportion of the CEO-specified spot quantity. The remaining perpetual PostOnly order continues within a bounded window, then its remainder is canceled. Duplicate private events cannot repeat spot orders; cumulative fills cannot exceed either CEO input; unknown execution or cancellation state stops further side effects.
- Fixed position, notional, daily-volume, batch-count and loss caps are not required for the founder-owned local test accounts. The former 1-ounce acceptance size is a test convention, not a permanent product limit. Instruction-bounded quantity, one-batch identity, cumulative-fill ceilings, bounded chase, global execution serialization, Kill Switch, fail-closed unknown handling, reconciliation and forced read-only reset remain mandatory. Existing Platform and Runtime fixed-notional-cap code remains a controlled-live readiness blocker; this alignment slice preserves it only for legacy compatibility and does not claim Live Write readiness.
- The initial repository and local-test parameters are owner-approved: 15-second total chase TTL, one-second evaluation cadence, at most five amend or cancel-repost mutations, and at least one tick of price change before mutation. These values are configuration, not exchange facts or permanent production defaults; changing them before controlled-live use requires evidence and owner approval.
- A persistent MT5 connector owns one ordinary-account Terminal session. Browser and Platform API reads do not log in or switch MT5 accounts per request. Future accounts use isolated connector instances.
- Only the CEO may initiate strategy open, close or risk-disposition commands through the website. Browser identity does not bypass Live Write, Kill Switch, idempotency, reconciliation or Runtime gates.
- Existing frontend layout, visual hierarchy and interaction style are frozen by default. Changes are limited to necessary real-data wiring, state disclosure and approved trading controls; material redesign returns to the owner.
- 0.11.1 is local acceptance only. Server deployment, production operation and unattended trading are excluded.
