# Platform 0.8.0

Release date: 2026-07-26

## Release scope

Platform 0.8.0 packages the completed engineering work from responsive-layout standardization through the market-only cross-spread lifecycle, one-ounce live-acceptance hardening and read-only live account observability.

This is a backward-compatible minor release. It adds substantial execution, close, history, risk and frontend capabilities while retaining maintained compatibility surfaces and leaving the Platform–Runtime V1 compatibility version independent.

## Execution and lifecycle

- Bybit market orders require terminal fill confirmation before MT5 hedge submission.
- MT5 hedge quantity derives from the confirmed Bybit fill and current MT5 contract size, minimum and step.
- Bybit market close validates the matching live position and submits `reduceOnly=true` with the correct `positionIdx`.
- MT5 market close binds an explicit Position Ticket and rejects side, symbol, ticket or quantity ambiguity.
- Confirmed hedged opens create persistent exit plans.
- LONG_SPREAD and SHORT_SPREAD TP/SL evaluate the executable close spread rather than the nominal open spread.
- Atomic exit-plan claims prevent duplicate automatic and manual close submission.

## Live acceptance hardening

- Current Venue specifications are read immediately before live cross-spread submission.
- Runtime independently enforces the temporary 1 troy ounce acceptance ceiling after MT5 lot-to-ounce conversion.
- At most one non-closed cross-spread lifecycle is allowed during the acceptance phase.
- New opens are blocked by external exposure, unresolved batches, manual intervention or non-closed exit plans.
- A completed open or close requires verification of the real external positions.
- A definitive MT5 hedge failure may trigger one idempotent Bybit reduce-only rollback only after exact external-exposure proof.
- Unknown MT5 outcomes never authorize blind retry or automatic rollback.

## Order, fill and account observability

- Route-independent Bybit and MT5 current-order reads remain available after Runtime restart or local Route loss.
- Historical Order and Fill/Deal queries use explicit bounded time windows and deterministic continuation.
- One history request is limited to seven days and 100 records per page.
- Bybit history uses Venue cursor continuation; MT5 uses deterministic offset continuation inside a fixed window.
- Order diagnostics include remaining quantity, reduce-only intent, target identity, time-in-force and rejection/cancellation evidence where available.
- Bybit positions expose Venue-reported mark, break-even, margin, leverage, risk and finite liquidation-price fields.
- MT5 does not fabricate a per-position liquidation price; account Margin Level, Margin Call and Stop Out are exposed instead.
- The Platform provides a two-Venue aggregate with independent `complete`, `partial` and `unavailable` section states.
- The frontend includes a read-only live acceptance dashboard for account risk, positions, active/recent orders and recent fills.
- The acceptance quantity defaults to 1 oz.

## Frontend foundation

- Responsive-layout ownership, Page Shell rules and cross-viewport acceptance are documented.
- The homepage uses a bounded desktop frame, deterministic Hero reflow and stable dashboard density.
- The retained legacy market/limit execution card remains read-only while the bounded market lifecycle panel owns real market actions.

## Compatibility retained

- The deprecated Backend `POST /api/v1/trading/orders` compatibility endpoint remains available.
- `trading.submit_order` remains a compatibility delegate.
- Existing FinancialFact, reconciliation, accounting and database authority remain unchanged.
- FastAPI metadata and Platform–Runtime contract versions describe component/API compatibility and are not required to equal `0.8.0`.

## Safety defaults unchanged

```text
TradingMode=simulation
Gateway=fake
Platform Live Write=false
Runtime Live Write=false
Cross-spread Exit Monitor=false
Cross-spread acceptance max quantity=1 oz
Cross-spread non-closed lifecycle max=1
```

No version update enables real trading, increases limits or starts the automatic exit monitor.

## Operational acceptance boundary

Platform 0.8.0 records engineering completion, not proof of real Bybit/MT5 operation. Issue #39 remains responsible for Windows-host evidence covering credentials, permissions, broker fields, Terminal stability, history retention, repeated supervised 1 oz Open/Close cycles, rollback/manual takeover, Kill Switch drills and end-of-day reconciliation.

Private Bybit WebSocket confirmation, real spread-limit execution, limit TP/SL and any relaxation of temporary acceptance limits remain separate future scopes.
